We have conducted a systematic review and are in the process of conducting a meta-analysis synthesizes evidence on the benefits of web decorations in spiders across experimental and observational studies. It examines how decorations influence key outcomes such as prey attraction, predator avoidance, and survival. By standardizing results across studies, the project aims to quantify the overall effect of decoration and identify the ecological contexts in which it is most advantageous. The findings should help clarify what is already known about web-decorating behavior and why it might have evolved and its functions.
